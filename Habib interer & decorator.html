Common questions

Do you charge for the site visit?

No. The measurement and advice visit in Toba Tek Singh is free.

How long does the work take?

It depends on the size of the job. The finish date is written on your quotation.

Do you work outside Toba Tek Singh?

Yes, including Gojra, Kamalia, Pir Mahal and nearby areas. Call us to confirm your location.

Can a PVC door fit my existing frame?

In most cases, yes. We check your frame during the visit and tell you.

How does payment work?

An advance, then the balance on completion, as agreed in the written quotation.
